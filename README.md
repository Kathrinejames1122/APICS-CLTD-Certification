[![ASCM APICS CLTD](https://img.shields.io/badge/ASCM-APICS%20CLTD-005A8B?style=for-the-badge)](https://www.ascm.org/learning-development/certifications-credentials/cltd/)

A dedicated study guide and professional reference for candidates preparing for the **APICS Certified in Logistics, Transportation and Distribution (CLTD) Certification Exam**, covering logistics strategy, inventory, warehousing, transportation, global logistics, and distribution management.

---

## Exam Overview

The APICS CLTD certification validates knowledge and skills across logistics, transportation, and distribution operations, including the planning and movement of goods through global supply chains.

- **Exam Format**: Computer-based, multiple-choice examination; delivered at Pearson VUE test centers and online through OnVUE.
- **Target Audience**: Logistics, transportation, distribution, warehouse, inventory, fulfillment, and supply chain professionals.
- **Prerequisites**: No formal education or work-experience prerequisites are required by ASCM.

---

## Core Exam Domains & Knowledge Areas

### 1. Understand Logistics Fundamentals and Strategy

- Logistics principles, supply chain structures, and strategic alignment.
- Logistics processes, performance measures, and operational objectives.
- Technology, risk, and continuous improvement in logistics.

### 2. Conduct Capacity Planning and Demand Management

- Demand planning, forecasting, and demand variability.
- Capacity requirements and resource planning.
- Coordination of demand and logistics capacity.

### 3. Conduct Order Management

- Customer order processes and order fulfillment requirements.
- Order processing, service levels, and customer communication.
- Managing order flow and logistics performance.

### 4. Facilitate Inventory Management

- Inventory planning, control, and replenishment.
- Inventory policies, costs, and performance measures.
- Managing inventory availability and supply requirements.

### 5. Conduct Warehouse Distribution / Fulfillment Center Management

- Warehouse and fulfillment center operations.
- Storage, handling, picking, packing, and shipping processes.
- Warehouse performance, technology, and process improvement.

### 6. Conduct Transportation Management

- Transportation planning, execution, and carrier selection.
- Transportation modes, rates, and service performance.
- Shipment visibility, routing, and transportation technology.

### 7. Facilitate Global Logistics

- International trade, global logistics, and documentation.
- Customs, compliance, and cross-border transportation.
- Global logistics risks and international supply chain coordination.

### 8. Facilitate Logistics Network Design

- Logistics network structures and facility location decisions.
- Distribution channels, network optimization, and trade-offs.
- Evaluating logistics costs, service levels, and network performance.

### 9. Understand Sustainability and Reverse Logistics

- Sustainable logistics practices and environmental considerations.
- Reverse logistics, returns, reuse, and recovery processes.
- Risk, resilience, and responsible supply chain operations.
